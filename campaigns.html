Zeeenah Initiative – FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022™

A public initiative launched ahead of the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022™,
encouraging schools and public entities to decorate their building façades and
surrounding spaces. The campaign aimed to bring the World Cup atmosphere
across the city and create a welcoming and festive environment for residents and
visitors.

My Role: Visual Identity, Art Direction, Graphic Design & Campaign Visual Development

The colour palette was designed to give Zeeenah a vibrant and celebratory feel.

Launch event branding developed as part of the Zeeenah visual identity.

The Zeeenah identity brought to life at the official launch event.

Supporting campaign materials provided participants with ready-to-use designs and clear guidelines for bringing
the initiative to life.
