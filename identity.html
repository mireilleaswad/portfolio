Public Works Authority Brand Identity Refresh

Overview:
The Public Works Authority Brand Identity Refresh was a strategic initiative to modernise the organisation’s visual identity while preserving its established recognition. As the client-side Senior Graphic Designer, I represented the Public Works Authority throughout the project, collaborating with the appointed design agency, evaluating creative proposals and leading the implementation of the refreshed identity across corporate communications, publications, environmental graphics and digital platforms.

Client:
Public Works Authority (Ashghal), Qatar

Year:
2025–2026

Role:
Senior Graphic Designer

Scope:
Brand Identity • Corporate Communications • Publications • Signage • Environmental Graphics • Digital Assets

My Role:
As the client-side Senior Graphic Designer, I represented the Public Works Authority throughout the brand identity refresh initiative. I collaborated with the appointed design agency, reviewed creative proposals, evaluated deliverables and ensured alignment with the organisation’s objectives and brand standards.

Following approval of the refreshed identity, I led the implementation across corporate communications, publications, signage, environmental graphics, digital assets and branded templates, ensuring consistent application throughout the organisation.

The refreshed identity established a consistent visual system across corporate communications, publications, signage, digital platforms and environmental graphics, creating a cohesive brand experience across the organisation.

The refreshed colour palette establishes a modern visual language while maintaining the authority and recognisability of the Public Works Authority brand.

Brand Applications

The refreshed identity was translated across corporate, digital and environmental touchpoints
to create a consistent brand experience.
